LadderRush Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

LadderRush is a mobile game developed as an independent project. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use LadderRush.

Information We Collect

LadderRush does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to play the game.

The game may use third-party advertising services, including Google AdMob. These services may collect certain information from your device for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes, subject to your consent and the privacy policies of those providers.

Advertising

LadderRush uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may use device identifiers and other information to provide and measure advertisements.

Where required, LadderRush requests consent before using personal data for personalized advertising. You may be presented with privacy choices when using the game.

Third-Party Services

LadderRush may use third-party services that process information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

For information about Google's handling of data, please refer to Google's privacy documentation.

Children's Privacy

LadderRush is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through the game. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or LadderRush, you may contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's store listing.
